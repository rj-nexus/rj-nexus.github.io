Report unacceptable behavior

If someone in an RJ Nexus space has made you or others feel unsafe, harassed or unwelcome, tell us. A person reads every report, keeps it private and answers it.

Email your report to

rjnexus@hotmail.com

Write the email

Write in the language you are most comfortable with. You don’t need proof or the right words to report.

Where this applies

The Code of Conduct covers the RJ Nexus repositories, issues and discussions on GitHub, this documentation site, and any other space where someone speaks for the project.

What to include

  • Where it happened: a link, a screenshot or the name of the channel.
  • Who was involved, as far as you know.
  • What happened, in your own words, and roughly when.
  • Whether you want a reply, and how to reach you.

Leave out anything you are not comfortable sharing. A short report is still a report.

What happens after you report

  1. We confirmYou get a reply within 3 days saying that your report arrived and who is handling it.
  2. We look into itWe read what you sent and, when needed, ask you or others for more context. Only the people who need to know see the report.
  3. We decideWe choose the smallest response that stops the behavior and protects the people affected.
  4. We tell youYou hear what was decided, as far as privacy allows, usually within 14 days. If it takes longer, we say why and when to expect news.
  5. We keep a private recordNotes are stored privately so repeated problems are noticed. They are not shared.

Possible outcomes

ReminderA private message explaining what was wrong and what is expected.
WarningA written warning that says what happens if the behavior continues.
Temporary restrictionNo posting, commenting or contributing in RJ Nexus spaces for a set period.
Permanent removalA ban from all RJ Nexus spaces, used for serious or repeated violations.

The outcome depends on the harm done, not on who is involved. Maintainers and long-time contributors follow the same rules as everyone else.

Your privacy and safety

  • We don’t share your name or details with the person you reported without your permission, unless the law requires it.
  • Retaliating against someone who reported in good faith is a violation on its own.
  • If you disagree with a decision, reply to the same email and ask for a second look.

If the report is about the maintainer

If your report concerns the person who reads this inbox, you can report content on GitHub directly through GitHub’s abuse reporting form.

Looking for something else?

  • Found a security vulnerability? Follow the security policy and don’t open a public issue.
  • Found a bug or want a feature? Open an issue.
  • Have a question? Ask in Discussions.
